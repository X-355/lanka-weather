Rooftop solar estimator

A rough yearly and monthly output estimate for a rooftop system, from satellite-derived sunlight records. Use it to get a feel for the numbers. A proper site survey should come first.

Expected output by month

Output ≈ kWp × daily sunlight (kWh/m²) × performance ratio × orientation × shading. Tariffs and net-metering rules change, so enter the rate you're actually paid or charged. Cost and payback ignore financing, panel degradation (~0.5%/yr) and inverter replacement.
