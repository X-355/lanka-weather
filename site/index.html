No weather radar here yet. No public radar covers Sri Lanka, so this tab (like the big weather sites) uses satellite pictures and satellite rain estimates. They're good for seeing storm systems approach, but coarser than radar. Sri Lanka's first Doppler weather radar is being installed in Puttalam; if the Department of Meteorology shares its feed, it would go here.

Rivers & reservoirs

The 10-day outlook for Sri Lanka's main rivers at their gauging stations, and how much rain is falling and forecast upstream of the major reservoirs.

For official river levels and flood warnings, use the Irrigation Department's rivernet.lk. Spill-gate openings are announced by the Irrigation Department and the Mahaweli Authority. Disaster warnings: Disaster Management Centre, hotline 117. This page is a model-based outlook, not a warning.

Main rivers

Modelled flow (Copernicus GloFAS) at each station: now, and the highest expected in the next 10 days compared with the long-term average and the wettest year on record for that date. Click a row for the chart.

Reservoir watch

Average rain over each reservoir's catchment: the last 3 days and the next 3 days (forecast). Heavy rain upstream fills reservoirs and makes spill-gate openings more likely, but whether and when gates open is decided by the engineers operating them. The categories are our rule of thumb, not official thresholds. Reservoir positions are approximate.

Rooftop solar estimator

A rough yearly and monthly output estimate for a rooftop system, from satellite-derived sunlight records. Use it to get a feel for the numbers. A proper site survey should come first.

Expected output by month

Output ≈ kWp × daily sunlight (kWh/m²) × performance ratio × orientation × shading. Tariffs and net-metering rules change, so enter the rate you're actually paid or charged. Cost and payback ignore financing, panel degradation (~0.5%/yr) and inverter replacement.
